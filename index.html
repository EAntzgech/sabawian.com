About Sabawian Production

More Than A Decade of Visual Storytelling

Sabawian Production is an independent creative studio dedicated to producing compelling visual experiences through film, visual effects, storytelling, and emerging technologies.

With over ten years of hands-on production and post-production leadership across film production, video editing, visual effects, motion graphics, and commercial campaigns, our studio unites disciplined cinematic storytelling with modern computational artistry.
